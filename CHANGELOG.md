# Changelog

## tdp v0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

First release of terminu design and the terminu design principle, grown out of VTP
(see [vtp/](vtp/)).

- Principle P0–P6, Rules K / M / L / C / F / X / T, Family defaults D1–D7
- New relative to VTP: three scopes of operation with `global operation` (P3, M2, M4),
  K2, K9, M6, L4, L5, F7
- Letter hotkeys and symbol vocabulary explicitly out of scope (P5)
