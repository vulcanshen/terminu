# confirm (confirmation)

**Language**: English · [繁體中文](confirm-zh_TW.md)

## Purpose

One question, `Enter` accepting and `Esc` cancelling, saying what follows (F6): before deleting, before quitting, "Connect to X?". What to read before answering may sit above the question, scrolled with `j`/`k` when long (F1).

## Keys

- `Enter` accepts, `Esc` cancels, and it says what follows (Rules F6).
- Bottom-border hint: `Enter:<verb> Esc:cancel` (e.g. `Enter:delete Esc:cancel`). (Moved here from defaults D3 on
  2026-10-07.)
