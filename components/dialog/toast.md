# toast (a one-line message)

**Language**: English · [繁體中文](toast-zh_TW.md)

## Purpose

A one-line message popping up from the bottom, gone on `Esc` or when its time is up; every key but `Esc` passes through it (F1, F3): "Copied", a failed operation.

## Look

- Fixed at the bottom of the screen, shown for 2200 ms (`Esc` dismisses it at once, F3). (Moved here from
  defaults D3 on 2026-10-07.)
